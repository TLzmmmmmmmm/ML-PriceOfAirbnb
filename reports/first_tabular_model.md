## Method

Each fold independently fits the Step 5 preprocessor on four training folds, transforms the held-out fold, trains a `HistGradientBoostingRegressor`, and predicts only the held-out records. Every training record therefore receives one prediction from a model that did not train on that record.

The model uses squared-error loss, learning rate `0.05`, at most 500 boosting iterations, 31 leaf nodes per tree, minimum 20 samples per leaf, L2 regularization `1.0`, and early stopping with seed `151`.

The encoded feature count differs by fold because one-hot category vocabularies are fitted independently on each fold's training rows. The validation and test matrices generated by a given fitted preprocessor always use that preprocessor's fixed schema.
