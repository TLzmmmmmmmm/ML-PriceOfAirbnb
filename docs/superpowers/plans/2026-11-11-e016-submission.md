- Preserve the E016 feature set and E003 HGB configuration.
- Generate exactly the `Id` and `Predicted` columns in original test order.

- Keep the fitted model and submission CSV out of Git.

## Review Focus

- Full training must include both approved location columns and the E014 amenities features.
- Predictions must align exactly with the test row order.
- Negative, missing, or non-finite predictions must not reach the final CSV.
- The dedicated E016 path must not retrain unrelated E009/E012/E014 components.
- Aggregate records must match the generated CSV and model artifact.
